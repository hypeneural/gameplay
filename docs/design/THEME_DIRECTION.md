# Christmas Photography Premium

Photography is the protagonist. Shared tokens use deep cranberry, pine green, warm gold and snow-white contrast; the visual direction avoids generic cartoon Christmas art. Theme Lab is the rendering review surface for portrait/landscape frames, HUD, controls and victory treatment.

Use LOW, NORMAL and HIGH quality profiles. LOW removes expensive visual effects first; a realtime blur of a large customer photograph remains out of scope until benchmarked on target mobile devices.
